Design principles

Every screen inherits the same tokens, the same rounded language, and the same accent gradient. What changes is the density and the role of colour — Now Playing and Lyrics let the artwork bleed into the chrome, Home and Library keep the chrome quiet and let the content lead.

Motion
  • Waveform breathes only while playing
  • Album gradient crossfades on track change
  • Mini player slides up from behind the nav
  • Reduced-motion respected globally
Hierarchy
  • Display face reserved for identity and titles
  • Body face carries everything readable
  • Mono for timecodes, followers, kbps
  • Muted grey scale for secondary metadata
Accessibility
  • Contrast targets AAA on primary text
  • Touch targets ≥ 44dp on every control
  • All controls carry semantic labels
  • Focus ring visible on every interactive element
Native mapping
  • Media3 MediaSessionService drives playback
  • Single StateFlow is the source of truth
  • Compose Canvas draws the waveform live
  • Room persists favourites, recents, playlists
